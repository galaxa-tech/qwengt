Body & interface — Inter 400–700

Tier 02 — 16–18px at 1.5–1.6 · never above 32px · no Light or Thin · HEADING 24–32 Inter Bold

Data & labels — JetBrains Mono · medium · caps · +10% · 9–12px

Tier 03 — edge labels, tags, spec grid · never sentences · wide-moment alternatives: Archivo Black or Bebas Neue (one per project, never both)
