Privacy

A plain summary of how Galaxa Tech handles the information you send us. This is a general policy and will be updated after business registration and legal review.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send through our forms or messages: your name, email address, website address, business details and any message you write. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we also see the details you share there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, such as a free website audit or a quote.
  • To keep a record of your enquiry so we can follow up.

We do not sell your information.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored securely using Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Messages you send through WhatsApp or email are handled by those services under their own policies.
