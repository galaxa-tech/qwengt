Before and after

A simple example of how one workflow changes. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Before: manual

  1. A new enquiry arrives by message or email.
  2. Someone copies the details into a sheet.
  3. Someone remembers to reply.
  4. The follow-up gets forgotten.

After: automated

  1. The enquiry arrives and is captured automatically.
  2. Details are saved in the right place.
  3. An instant reply goes out.
  4. The follow-up is scheduled for you.
